READ BEFORE RELYING ON ANY RESULT

Limitations

  • Risk results are experimental screenings, not calibrated flood probabilities or official warnings.
  • Terrain-backed demonstration coverage is limited to a small pilot area. Outside that footprint, the map hides the demonstration layer and does not provide local terrain-based risk.
  • The bundled terrain and hotspot examples are simulated. They do not represent verified elevation, drainage capacity, or observed waterlogging.
  • Forecast guidance comes from Open-Meteo when available. No live IMD warning feed, municipal drainage inventory, or verified flood-event dataset is integrated.
  • Historical flood context is a small, source-linked starter set with selected reports in Andhra Pradesh, Odisha, Punjab, Assam, Himachal Pradesh, Jammu and Kashmir, Bihar, and Delhi—not complete India-wide coverage. It screens only the selected point, not a route ahead. Past reports and rain do not confirm current flooding or road closures; no matching record means unknown, not safe.
  • Community experiences are unverified submissions and are not used as ground truth or evidence of model accuracy.
  • Always follow official IMD / NDMA advisories and local authority instructions.
